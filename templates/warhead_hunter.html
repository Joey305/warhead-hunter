WARHEAD HUNTER PROTOCOL

Provide a target label + an RCSB search phrase. FASTA is optional but improves specificity when matching proteins.
START HERE — WHAT THIS TOOL SEARCHES
Warhead Hunter searches RCSB for ligand-bound structures matching your phrase, then maps solvent exposure around their ligands. It does not search a target name alone: the query should describe a protein plus a bound ligand, inhibitor, or covalent state.
1. Name the targetA short label for your results, e.g. CRBN.
2. Describe the structureUse a searchable RCSB phrase, e.g. cereblon pomalidomide bound.
3. Add FASTA if neededRecommended for broad names or protein families; then launch the scan.
